The Science

Backed by real
agronomic research.

Every recommendation is grounded in peer-reviewed science and established agricultural formulas.

Water Requirement (FAO-56)
ET₀ = (0.408·Δ·Rn + γ·(900/T+273)·u₂·vpd) / (Δ + γ·(1 + 0.34·u₂))

The gold-standard formula for calculating crop water needs, used by agricultural agencies worldwide.

Smart Task Balancing
ℒ = ∑ᵢ [ exp(−sᵢ)·ℒᵢ + sᵢ ] where sᵢ = log σᵢ²

Our AI automatically balances crop, fertilizer, and water predictions so no single task dominates learning.

Rare Crop Detection
FL(pₚ) = −α·(1−pₚ)^γ·log(pₚ)

Focal Loss ensures even rare and unusual crop varieties get predicted accurately, not just common ones.

Soil Nutrient Balance
NBS = (N/N̄ · P/P̄ · K/K̄)^⅓

Measures how balanced your soil nutrients are. Drives fertilizer recommendations to correct deficiencies.
