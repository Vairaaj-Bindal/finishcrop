How It Works

Soil to recommendation in three steps.

No agronomist needed. Place the sensor and follow the report.

01

Deploy the sensor

Push the probes 20cm into your soil. The device begins reading nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, pH, temperature, humidity, and rainfall every 15 minutes.

02

AI processes your data

Three models — XGBoost, Random Forest, and a multi-task Transformer — cross-reference your readings against 120,000 training samples.

03

Act on precise advice

Receive a clear report: best crop match, exact fertilizer type and dosage, and a weekly water schedule calibrated to your field conditions.

Science

Grounded in peer-reviewed research.

Every recommendation uses established agronomic formulas and validated ML techniques.

FAO-56 Penman-Monteith
ET₀ = (0.408·Δ·Rn + γ·(900/T+273)·u₂·vpd) / (Δ + γ·(1 + 0.34·u₂))

Reference evapotranspiration. The standard used by agricultural agencies worldwide for water calculations.

Uncertainty-Weighted MTL
ℒ = ∑ᵢ [ exp(−sᵢ)·ℒᵢ + sᵢ ]   sᵢ = log σᵢ²

Kendall et al. 2018. Automatically balances crop, fertilizer, and water losses via learned task uncertainty.

Focal Loss
FL(pₚ) = −α·(1−pₚ)^γ·log(pₚ)

Lin et al. 2017. Handles extreme class imbalance across 1,920 crop varieties by focusing on hard examples.

Nutrient Balance Score
NBS = (N/N̄ · P/P̄ · K/K̄)^⅓

Geometric mean of normalized NPK. Drives fertilizer selection: Urea for N-deficient, DAP for P, MOP for K.
