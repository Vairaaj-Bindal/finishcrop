How It Works

Three steps. No expertise needed.

Place the sensor, wait for the analysis, act on the recommendations.

Step 01

Place the Sensor

Push the probes 20cm into your soil. The device starts reading NPK, pH, temperature, humidity, and rainfall every 15 minutes automatically.

Step 02

AI Analyzes Your Soil

Three AI models cross-reference your readings against 120,000 training samples to identify the optimal crop, fertilizer, and irrigation plan.

Step 03

Follow the Report

Receive a clear report with the best crop match, exact fertilizer type and dosage, and a weekly water schedule calibrated to your field.

Science

Grounded in peer-reviewed research.

Every recommendation uses established agronomic formulas and validated ML techniques.

FAO-56 Penman-Monteith
ET₀ = (0.408·Δ·Rn + γ·(900/T+273)·u₂·vpd) / (Δ + γ·(1 + 0.34·u₂))

Reference evapotranspiration. The standard formula used by agricultural agencies worldwide for water requirement calculations.

Uncertainty-Weighted MTL
ℒ = ∑ᵢ [ exp(−sᵢ)·ℒᵢ + sᵢ ]   sᵢ = log σᵢ²

Kendall et al. 2018. Automatically balances crop, fertilizer, and water prediction losses via learned uncertainty.

Focal Loss
FL(pₚ) = −α·(1−pₚ)^γ·log(pₚ)

Lin et al. 2017. Handles extreme class imbalance across 1,920 crop varieties by down-weighting easy examples.

Nutrient Balance Score
NBS = (N/N̄ · P/P̄ · K/K̄)^⅓

Geometric mean of normalized NPK. Drives fertilizer selection: Urea for N-deficient, DAP for P, MOP for K.
