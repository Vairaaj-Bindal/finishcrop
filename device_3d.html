Bill of Materials
Every component needed to build the AgroSense Pro unit. Prices are typical retail/AliExpress estimates for single-unit quantities.
01 · Microcontroller
ESP32-WROOM-32D Module
ESP32-WROOM-32D
Dual-core Xtensa LX6 @ 240 MHz, 4MB Flash, 520KB SRAM, integrated Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n + Bluetooth 4.2 BLE. Main controller for all sensor acquisition and wireless data upload.
Qty: 1 ~$4.50 MCU 3.3V / 5V VIN
02 · Soil Nutrient Sensor
RS485 NPK Soil Sensor
JXBS-3001-NPK-N01 (or equivalent)
Capacitive NPK sensor using RS485 / Modbus RTU protocol. Measures Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium in mg/kg (ppm). Supply: 5–30V DC. Range: 0–1999 mg/kg each nutrient. Response time <1 s.
Qty: 1 ~$28–45 Soil Sensor RS485 4800 baud
03 · RS485 Transceiver
MAX3485 Half-Duplex RS485 IC
MAX3485ESA+
3.3V-compatible RS485 transceiver (unlike MAX485 which needs 5V). Connects ESP32 UART2 to the NPK sensor's RS485 bus. DE pin tied to RE for half-duplex direction control via GPIO4.
Qty: 1 ~$0.80 Interface IC 3.3V supply ✓
04 · pH Sensor
Analog pH Electrode + Circuit Board
pH-4502C Module + BNC Glass Electrode
BNC pH electrode with signal conditioning board. Analog output 0–3.3V (0–14 pH). Powered at 5V; output divided with 1kΩ+2kΩ resistors to stay within ESP32 ADC 3.3V limit. Requires calibration with pH 4 and pH 7 buffer solutions.
Qty: 1 ~$6–12 Soil Sensor ADC GPIO34
05 · Temp/Humidity Sensor
DHT22 (AM2302) Temperature & Humidity
DHT22 / AM2302
Capacitive humidity + thermistor combo. Range: −40 to +80°C (±0.5°C), 0–100% RH (±2% RH). Single-wire serial protocol. Requires 10kΩ pull-up on data line. Placed in radiation shield on exterior of housing to measure ambient conditions.
Qty: 1 ~$3.50 Environmental GPIO26 + 10kΩ PU
06 · Soil Temperature Probe
DS18B20 Waterproof Temperature Probe
DS18B20 (TO-92 + stainless tube)
1-Wire digital thermometer in IP67 stainless steel waterproof tube (60cm cable). Measures soil temperature at root zone. Range: −55 to +125°C, ±0.5°C accuracy. 4.7kΩ pull-up required. Multiple sensors can share one GPIO.
Qty: 1 ~$4.00 Soil Sensor GPIO27 + 4.7kΩ PU
07 · Rainfall Sensor
Tipping Bucket Rain Gauge
Generic 0.2mm tipping bucket (WS-3000 equiv.)
Self-emptying tipping bucket mechanism. Each tip = 0.2 mm of rainfall. Reed switch output wired to GPIO25 with INPUT_PULLUP. Interrupt-driven counting. Weatherproof, UV-resistant housing. Mounting arm included.
Qty: 1 ~$18–30 Weather GPIO25 IRQ
08 · Display
0.96" SSD1306 OLED Display
SSD1306 128×64 I²C
Monochrome 128×64 OLED display, I²C interface (addr 0x3C). Shows real-time sensor readings and status. 3.3V compatible. Very low power (~20 mA active, <1 μA in sleep). Ideal for battery-powered devices.
Qty: 1 ~$2.50 Display I²C GPIO21/22
09 · Battery
18650 Li-ion Cell (Protected)
Samsung INR18650-25R or Panasonic NCR18650B
2500–3400 mAh 18650 Li-ion cell with built-in PCB protection (overcurrent, overcharge, deep discharge). 3.7V nominal. Provides ~16 hours continuous operation or 4+ days with deep-sleep firmware. Use protected cell only for safety.
Qty: 1 ~$5–8 Power 3.7V / 2500mAh
10 · Solar Charger IC
CN3065 Solar LiPo Charger Module
CN3065 + breakout board
Designed specifically for solar charging. Accepts 4.5–6.5V input (unlike TP4056 which needs regulated 5V). MPPT-like behaviour. Charge current set by external resistor (2kΩ = 500mA). LED indicators for charging/full. Protects against overvoltage from solar panel.
Qty: 1 ~$1.50 Power 4.5–6.5V solar in
11 · Boost Converter
MT3608 DC-DC Boost Module
MT3608 (2A, adjustable)
Boosts 3.7V battery voltage to stable 5V for ESP32 VIN and NPK sensor. 88% typical efficiency at 150mA load. Adjust output with onboard trimmer to exactly 5.00V before installation. Maximum continuous output: 2A. Input: 2–24V.
Qty: 1 ~$1.20 Power 3.7V → 5.0V
12 · Solar Panel
6V / 2W Monocrystalline Solar Panel
6V 333mA polycrystalline panel (90×60mm)
Voc=7.2V, Vmp=6V, Isc=333mA, Imp=280mA. Sized to fully recharge the 18650 within 8 hours of sunlight. Wires through a 1N5819 Schottky diode (0.3V drop) to prevent back-discharge at night. Epoxy-coated for outdoor use.
Qty: 1 ~$4–7 Power 6V / 2W
13 · Storage
Micro SD Card Module (SPI)
MH-SD Module or equivalent
SPI-interface microSD module with built-in 3.3V level shifter and LDO regulator. Logs all sensor readings with timestamps to CSV on the SD card for offline analysis. Use 2–8GB Class 10 card (not included). Supports FAT32.
Qty: 1 ~$1.50 Storage SPI GPIO18/19/23/5
14 · Memory Card
8GB Micro SD Card (Class 10)
SanDisk Ultra 8GB microSDHC
Pre-formatted FAT32. Stores sensor logs at 15-minute intervals → ~1MB/day → 8 years of storage. Use a name-brand card to avoid data corruption in extreme temperatures.
Qty: 1 ~$4.00 Storage FAT32
15 · Enclosure
IP67 Weatherproof ABS Enclosure
110×65×50mm ABS IP67 (Hammond 1555 equiv.)
Ingress protection IP67: dust-tight, 30-min submersion to 1m. ABS plastic, UV-stabilised. Rubber gasket seal. 4× M3 mounting screws. Dark green colour preferred to reduce solar heating. Drill holes for probe cable entry (use cable glands).
Qty: 1 ~$8–14 Mechanical IP67
16 · Custom PCB
AgroSense Pro Main PCB
Custom 2-layer FR4, 80×55mm
Custom PCB integrating all component footprints: ESP32 module, MAX3485, MT3608 boost circuit, CN3065 charger, sensor headers, power connectors, and battery holder. Designed in KiCad. 2-layer FR4, HASL finish, 1.6mm thickness. Order from JLCPCB or PCBWay.
Qty: 1 (min 5-pack) ~$2–4 each PCB FR4 2-layer
17 · Sensor Probes
Stainless Steel Insertion Probes (3×)
304 SS rod 6mm dia × 150mm
Three 304-grade stainless steel probes for soil insertion. One carries NPK sensor electrodes (connected to NPK sensor wire), one carries the DS18B20 capsule, one is reference ground. Epoxy-sealed probe entry into housing. Sharpened tip for easy insertion.
Qty: 3 ~$2 total Mechanical 304 SS
18 · Cable Glands
PG7 IP68 Nylon Cable Glands
PG7 M12 thread (3–6.5mm cable dia)
Watertight cable entry for sensor probe wires and solar panel cable. IP68 rated when fully tightened. 4 needed: NPK cable, rain gauge cable, solar panel cable, auxiliary port. Nylon body, stainless lock nut.
Qty: 4 ~$3 (pack of 10) Mechanical IP68 PG7
19 · Mounting Hardware
Galvanised Steel Mounting Stake
50cm × 20mm galvanised steel angle bar
L-shaped stake to position the device at proper height above ground (probes in soil, solar panel angled toward sun). Spray-painted with anti-rust primer. M4 bolt and wing nut for quick removal. Alternatively use a 25mm PVC conduit with end cap.
Qty: 1 ~$3–5 Mechanical Galvanised
20 · Passive Components
Resistors, Capacitors & Diodes
Assorted 0402 / through-hole
Resistors: 4.7kΩ×2 (DS18B20/I²C PU), 10kΩ×2 (DHT22 PU / rain PU), 1kΩ+2kΩ (pH divider), 330Ω (LED), 100kΩ×2 (battery divider), 2kΩ (CN3065 prog).
Capacitors: 100nF×4 (decoupling), 10μF×3 (power rails), 22μF×2 (MT3608 in/out).
Diodes: 1N5819 Schottky (solar anti-backflow), SS14 (MT3608 boost diode).
Assorted ~$2–4 Passives
21 · Connectors
JST-PH 2-pin + Pin Headers + Terminal Blocks
JST-PH 2.0mm + 2.54mm headers
JST-PH 2.0mm 2-pin connector for battery (standard 18650 holder). 2.54mm female headers for sensor connections. 3.5mm screw terminal blocks for rain gauge and solar panel. USB-C breakout board for programming and optional 5V charging input.
Assorted ~$2 Connectors
22 · Status Indicator
3mm Green LED + Lens Cap
Kingbright L-934LGD + IP67 lens cap
High-brightness 3mm green LED (550nm, 20mA, 40° viewing angle) with waterproof lens cap for panel mounting. Controlled via GPIO2 with 330Ω series resistor. Flash patterns: 1 blink=OK, 2 blinks=uploading, solid=charging, off=deep sleep.
Qty: 1 ~$0.50 Indicator GPIO2 + 330Ω
23 · Boost Inductor
22μH Shielded SMD Inductor
Würth 744025220 or TDK CLF6045 22μH
Required for MT3608 boost converter circuit. 22μH, 2A saturation current, DCR <150mΩ. Shielded design minimises EMI from switching. If using a pre-made MT3608 module, this is already included on the module PCB.
Qty: 1 ~$0.80 Magnetics 22μH / 2A
24 · Weatherproofing
Silicone Conformal Coating + Probe Potting Compound
MG Chemicals 419C + 2-part epoxy
Spray conformal coating on PCB to prevent condensation-induced shorts. 2-part waterproof epoxy to pot probe entry points and cable glands. Prevents moisture ingress in high-humidity or rain conditions. Apply after all testing is complete.
1 can + 1 tube ~$12 Materials Weatherproof
25 · Wiring
Silicone Hookup Wire + RS485 Twisted Pair
24 AWG silicone + 22 AWG twisted pair
Silicone-insulated 24 AWG wire (flexible, −60°C to +200°C) for internal connections. RS485 twisted pair cable (22 AWG shielded, 1m) from housing to NPK sensor — critical for noise immunity. Colour code: red=5V, black=GND, green=data, blue=RS485-A, white=RS485-B.
~2m assorted ~$3 Materials 24 AWG silicone
