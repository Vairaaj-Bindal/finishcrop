Bill of Materials
Every component needed to build the AgroSense Pro unit. Prices are typical retail/AliExpress estimates for single-unit quantities.
01 · Microcontroller
ESP32-C3 SuperMini Module
ESP32-C3 SuperMini
RISC-V 160 MHz, 4MB Flash, 400KB SRAM, integrated Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n + BLE 5.0. USB-C built-in for programming. Smaller and cheaper than WROOM-32D while retaining all required ADC, I²C, and interrupt pins.
Qty: 1~$2.00MCU3.3V / 5V via USB
02 · EC Soil Probes
Stainless Steel 316 Soil Probes (4×)
SS316 4mm rod × 150mm — DIY
4 stainless steel 316 rods (4mm dia, 150mm long) sharpened to a point. Two probes carry AC signal from 555 timer circuit; two measure EC voltage. Combined with pH, EC enables N/P/K nutrient estimation. Far cheaper than RS485 NPK sensors.
Qty: 4~$1.50Soil SensorAC-excited EC
03 · EC Oscillator IC
NE555 Timer IC (EC measurement)
NE555P (DIP-8)
Configured as astable oscillator (~1 kHz AC square wave) to drive EC probes. AC excitation prevents electrode polarisation that ruins DC measurements. Output voltage after soil divides is read by ESP32-C3 ADC on GPIO35. Powered from 3.3V rail.
Qty: 1~$0.20MeasurementGPIO14 enable
04 · pH Sensor
Analog pH Electrode + Board
pH-4502C Module + BNC Glass Electrode
BNC glass pH electrode with signal conditioning board. Analog output 0–3.3V (0–14 pH). Powered at 5V; output divided with 1kΩ+2kΩ to stay within ESP32 ADC 3.3V limit. Calibrate with pH 4 and pH 7 buffer solutions before deployment.
Qty: 1~$5.00Soil SensorADC GPIO34
05 · Temp + Humidity
AHT20 I²C Sensor
AHT20 (or DHT22 as alternative)
Capacitive humidity + temperature on I²C. Range: −40 to +85°C (±0.3°C), 0–100% RH (±2%). Cheaper and more accurate than DHT22. Shares I²C bus with OLED. 3.3V supply. Mount in radiation shield on device exterior for ambient conditions.
Qty: 1~$1.50EnvironmentalI²C GPIO21/22
06 · Soil Temperature
DS18B20 Waterproof Probe
DS18B20 (stainless tube, 60cm cable)
1-Wire digital thermometer in IP67 waterproof stainless tube. Measures soil temperature at root zone depth. Range: −55 to +125°C, ±0.5°C. 4.7kΩ pull-up required. One of the 4 soil probes carries this sensor embedded at tip.
Qty: 1~$1.80Soil Sensor1-Wire GPIO27
07 · Rain Gauge
DIY Tipping Bucket (Reed Switch)
Reed switch + neodymium magnet + 3D-printed bucket
DIY tipping bucket using a reed switch and magnet on the pivot arm. 3D-print the funnel and bucket from PETG (UV and weather resistant). Each tip = 0.2mm rain. Reed switch wired to GPIO25 with INPUT_PULLUP. Total cost vs commercial: $3 vs $18–30.
Qty: 1~$3.00WeatherGPIO25 IRQ
08 · Display
0.96" SSD1306 OLED (I²C)
SSD1306 128×64
Monochrome 128×64 OLED, I²C at 0x3C. Shows real-time NPK estimate, pH, temp, humidity, battery level, and upload status. 3.3V compatible. <1 μA in sleep. Shares I²C bus with AHT20 sensor.
Qty: 1~$1.80DisplayI²C 0x3C
09 · Battery
18650 Li-ion Cell (2600mAh)
Generic protected 18650 2600mAh
3.7V nominal, 2600mAh protected Li-ion cell. Provides ~22 hours continuous or 6+ days in deep-sleep mode. Use a protected cell (built-in PCB) to prevent overcharge/overdischarge damage. AliExpress unbranded protected cells are adequate for this use.
Qty: 1~$2.50Power3.7V 2600mAh
10 · Battery Charger
TP4056 Solar Charging Module
TP4056 with DW01A protection (micro-USB or USB-C)
Li-ion charger IC rated for up to 5.5V input — solar-compatible modules with an added MOSFET gate. 1A charge rate. Use the variant with integrated protection (DW01A + FS8205). Input from 5.5V solar panel via 1N5819 Schottky diode to clamp voltage.
Qty: 1~$0.60PowerUp to 5.5V in
11 · Boost Converter
MT3608 DC-DC Boost Module
MT3608 adjustable boost (pre-built module)
Boosts 3.7V battery to stable 5V for pH module and ESP32 VIN. 88% efficiency @ 100mA load. Pre-built module includes the 22μH inductor and SS14 diode — no separate sourcing required. Pre-adjust trimmer to exactly 5.00V before installation.
Qty: 1~$0.60Power3.7V → 5.0V
12 · Solar Panel
5.5V / 1W Epoxy Solar Panel
5.5V 180mA monocrystalline epoxy panel (110×69mm)
Voc=6.8V, Vmp=5.5V, Isc=200mA. Wire via 1N5819 Schottky diode to TP4056 input. In full sun, charges from 0% to full in ~14 hours. Net positive charging in 4+ hrs/day sunlight. Epoxy laminated for outdoor durability. Attach to lid with weatherproof adhesive tape.
Qty: 1~$2.50Power5.5V / 1W
13 · Custom PCB
AgroSense PCB (2-layer, 70×50mm)
2-layer FR4 1.6mm HASL — JLCPCB
Integrates ESP32-C3 SuperMini, 555 timer EC circuit, pH divider, DS18B20 pull-up, AHT20 I²C, OLED header, battery connector, and boost module header. Order 5 for $10 from JLCPCB (~$2/each). Design in EasyEDA or KiCad. Gerbers ready to export.
Qty: 1 (min 5)~$2.00 eaPCBFR4 2-layer
14 · Enclosure
IP65 ABS Weatherproof Box
120×80×50mm ABS IP65 (generic)
Dust-tight, jet-water resistant (IP65). ABS plastic with rubber gasket. Drill 4× holes in bottom for probe pass-through (use waterproof epoxy seal), 1× hole for cable gland (solar panel wire). IP65 is sufficient for field use — full submersion not required.
Qty: 1~$3.50MechanicalIP65
15 · Cable Glands
M12 Nylon Cable Glands (PG7)
PG7 IP67 nylon — 3–6.5mm cable dia
Watertight cable entry for solar panel cable and rain gauge cable. 2 needed. IP67 rating. Tighten to compress rubber insert around cable. Available in packs of 10 for ~$2 on AliExpress — keep extras for future builds.
Qty: 2~$0.80MechanicalIP67 PG7
16 · Mounting Stake
25mm PVC Conduit Stake (50cm)
25mm OD PVC electrical conduit + end cap
Half-metre section of 25mm PVC conduit hammered into ground. Attach device enclosure to conduit with stainless M4 hose clamps. PVC is UV-stable, rust-free, and cheap. End cap keeps dirt out. Reposition by simply pulling up and re-inserting.
Qty: 1~$1.50MechanicalPVC
17 · Status LED
5mm RGB LED (Common Cathode)
5mm CC RGB LED + IP67 lens cap
RGB LED for multi-state status indication: green=OK/uploading, amber=charging, red=error, blue=BLE active. Three 330Ω series resistors on R/G/B channels. Waterproof lens cap for panel mounting. Controlled by ESP32-C3 GPIO2 (green channel).
Qty: 1~$0.30IndicatorGPIO2 + 3×330Ω
18 · Passives
Resistors, Capacitors & Diodes
Through-hole / 0402 SMD assorted
Resistors: 4.7kΩ (DS18B20/I²C PU), 10kΩ×3 (AHT20/rain/EC divider), 1kΩ+2kΩ (pH divider), 100kΩ×2 (battery divider), 330Ω×3 (LED), 10kΩ+10kΩ (EC divider).
Capacitors: 100nF×4 (decoupling), 10μF×2 (power rails).
Diodes: 1N5819 Schottky×2 (solar + EC rectifier).
Assorted~$1.50Passives

JST-PH 2-pin + Pin Headers + Terminal Blocks
JST-PH 2.0mm + 2.54mm + 3.5mm terminals
JST-PH 2.0mm for 18650 battery. 2.54mm female headers for probe connections to PCB. 3.5mm screw terminals for rain gauge and solar panel (removable for transport). All connections labelled on PCB silkscreen.
Assorted~$1.00Connectors

2-part epoxy + 24 AWG silicone hookup wire
2-part waterproof epoxy to seal probe entry holes in enclosure bottom and around cable gland threads. 24 AWG silicone-insulated wire (flexible, −60°C to +200°C) for internal connections. 1m assorted colours. Silicone resists humidity better than PVC insulation.
